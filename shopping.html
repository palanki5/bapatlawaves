Local craft and essentials

  • Beach essentials, towels and casual items near the town center
  • Small shops with practical travel and souvenir picks
  • Great for quick purchases before leaving the coast

Souvenirs

  • Local keepsakes to remember your coastal trip
  • Pick up small gifts and beach-themed items
  • Best found in local markets and roadside shops

Sweet shops

  • Regional sweets and snacks from local confectioners
  • Good treats to carry back home for family and friends
  • Look for shops with fresh batches and local favorites

Shopping tips

  • Carry cash for small markets and roadside vendors
  • Bargain politely when shopping in local stalls
  • Buy only from trusted shops for food and packaged goods
