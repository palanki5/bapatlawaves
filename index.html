Beach escapes

Find your favorite stretch of sand beaches

Suryalanka Beach

A lively shoreline with long stretches of sand, early morning calm and a popular promenade.

  • Best time: Nov–Feb
  • Distance: 12 km

Pandurangapuram Beach

Peaceful and scenic, this stretch offers a more relaxed coastal experience with wide-open sea views.

  • Best time: Nov–Jan
  • Distance: 9 km

Ramapuram Beach

A quieter fishing-community coastline where visitors can enjoy clean sands and a slower, more local feel.

  • Best time: Oct–Feb
  • Distance: 16 km

Vadarevu Beach

A quieter shore with a calm ambience and wide-open views that are perfect for a slow morning.

  • Best time: Nov–Jan
  • Distance: 18 km

Odarevu Beach

One of the quieter coastal spots, ideal for relaxing walks, sunset views and a gentler beach atmosphere.

  • Best time: Nov–Feb
  • Distance: 14 km

Travel details

Plan your Bapatla getaway

How to reach

  • By road: Well connected via NH 16 and local highways from Vijayawada, Guntur and Chennai.
  • By rail: Bapatla and nearby stations offer easy access for scenic train journeys.
  • Nearest airport: Vijayawada Airport is the closest major airport, about 3–4 hours away by road.

Best season & stay

  • Best season: October to March for cooler weather and comfortable beach walks.
  • Stay options: Choose from family-friendly stays, beach resorts and budget guesthouses near the coast.
  • Safety tips: Swim only in designated areas, keep an eye on children and avoid late-night isolated stretches.
