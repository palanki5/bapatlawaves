Disaster recovery guidance

  • Follow local advisories during storms, floods or rough coastal conditions
  • Stay away from unsafe shoreline areas during strong weather warnings
  • Keep emergency supplies and basic first aid ready during travel

Travel safety tips

  • Travel with a charged phone and offline map access
  • Use trusted transport, especially after dark
  • Keep cash, passport and local emergency numbers in one safe place
