Preparedness

Disaster Recovery

Know how to respond calmly during weather alerts, strong sea conditions, or sudden local disruptions while staying near the coast.

Weather alerts

  • Monitor local advisories before heading to the shore during storms or heavy winds.
  • Avoid isolated stretches of beach when there are warnings or rough sea conditions.
  • Listen to local updates and follow the guidance of officials or accommodation staff.

Coastal safety

  • Stay clear of wave-swept areas during strong tides or sudden surges.
  • Keep children close and avoid long beach walks after dark in unsafe zones.
  • Carry a flashlight, water and a charged phone when traveling late.

Recovery steps

  • Move to a higher, safer location if an area is flooding or unstable.
  • Contact local emergency support and inform your hotel or group leader.
  • Document essentials and keep a backup of emergency numbers on paper too.

Travel preparation

  • Pack a small first-aid kit and keep basic medicines in your bag.
  • Save emergency contacts and route information in offline mode.
  • Plan flexible timings so you can avoid risky weather windows.
