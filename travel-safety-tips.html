Travel Safety Tips

Simple habits can make a big difference when traveling to the coast, especially with family or during the evening hours.

Before you go

  • Save your accommodation address, local emergency numbers and route details offline.
  • Carry a charged phone, power bank and a small first-aid kit.
  • Share your plan with family or friends before long beach trips.

On the beach

  • Swim only in observed, safe areas and never alone in rough water.
  • Keep valuables secure and avoid leaving bags unattended.
  • Watch children closely and keep clear of strong tide changes.

At night

  • Prefer well-lit roads and trusted transport after dark.
  • Avoid isolated shore stretches and poorly lit lanes after sunset.
  • Travel in groups when exploring unfamiliar beach areas late at night.

Emergency readiness

  • Keep important contact numbers on paper and in your phone.
  • Know the nearest clinic, ambulance, and police support point.
  • Stay alert during weather alerts and rough coastal conditions.
